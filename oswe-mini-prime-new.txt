## Why this fixes the crash (short) 💡

- The crash came from converting a negative int16_t (specifically the sentinel `-32768`) to `size_t` for indexing, which underflowed to a huge unsigned value and caused `vector::at` to throw.
- By returning an unsigned magnitude (safe negation into a wider signed type) and clamping the magnitude so the maximum value maps into the last bucket, we avoid the negative-to-unsigned underflow and keep the index in-range.
